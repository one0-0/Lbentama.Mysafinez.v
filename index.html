A unique tradition in Thailand

The Monkey
Buffet Festival

The world is full of unusual and exciting celebrations. One of the strangest is the Monkey Buffet Festival, which takes place in Lopburi, a city in Thailand.

📍 Lopburi, Thailand🐒 Since 1989

About the Festival

The festival was first held in 1989. Every year, on the last Sunday in November, a local hotel sponsors a festival for the resident macaque monkeys. In the Buddhist tradition, the Thai revere the monkey as a hero.

📅 When?Usually the last Sunday in November.
🍉 What happens?People prepare a huge feast of fruits and vegetables for the monkeys.
🎉 Why is it special?It celebrates a local tradition and attracts visitors to Lopburi.

Visitors and Tourism

The festival also attracts many tourists from different countries who come to watch the monkeys enjoying their feast. Visitors take pictures, watch the monkeys eating fruits and vegetables, and discover this unusual tradition. It is a funny and exciting event that makes Lopburi famous around the world.
